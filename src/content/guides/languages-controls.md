## Language coverage

The Steam listing shows seven listed languages. English, German, Russian and Simplified Chinese include full audio in the listing; the remaining language rows should be checked directly on Steam for their current interface and subtitle flags.

## Controllers

Steam lists full controller support, and the official FAQ states that PC controllers are supported. A complete button map is not published here until it is captured from the current client.
