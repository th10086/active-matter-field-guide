## Confirmed platform facts

The Steam build lists Windows and Linux support. The official FAQ also states native Linux and Steam Deck support. macOS is not listed in the Steam platform facts.

## Anti-cheat note

The Steam listing identifies BattlEye. Treat that as a compatibility consideration when checking security software, permissions or launch issues. Do not mistake anti-cheat information for a gameplay feature.

## Before you buy

Compare your operating system with the live Steam requirements and recheck after major patches. This page intentionally does not invent minimum GPU, CPU or memory values that are not present in the verified source record.
