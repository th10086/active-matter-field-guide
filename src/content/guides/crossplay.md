## Cross-platform multiplayer

The official FAQ confirms cross-platform multiplayer. The supported platforms are Steam, Gaijin.Net PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Cross-progression

The current FAQ confirms cross-progression between Steam and Gaijin.Net PC. It does not support console-to-PC cross-progression. This FAQ wording is the source of truth because a generic purchase modal makes a broader claim.

Early-version owners have an account-linking window ending November 23, 2026. Recheck the FAQ if the date has passed before repeating it.
