## Short answer

Yes. **Active Matter supports cross-platform multiplayer** on Steam, Gaijin.Net PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Cross-progression is more limited: the official FAQ confirms shared progress between **Steam and Gaijin.Net on PC**, but not between PC and console versions.

## Crossplay and progression table

| Feature | Supported? | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-platform multiplayer | Yes | Players on supported platforms can play across platform boundaries. |
| Steam ↔ Gaijin.Net PC progress | Yes | The official FAQ confirms shared PC progress. |
| PC ↔ PlayStation progress | No | The official FAQ says PC and console progress cannot be transferred. |
| PC ↔ Xbox progress | No | The same console-to-PC restriction applies. |

## Crossplay is not cross-progression

Crossplay answers **who you can play with**. Cross-progression answers **where your account progress follows you**. Active Matter supports broader multiplayer connections than progress transfer, so do not assume that playing with another platform also moves your inventory or progression there.

## Account linking

Use the current official account-linking flow when moving between Steam and Gaijin.Net PC. Do not rely on old linking deadlines or screenshots: account windows and interface steps can change after an update.
