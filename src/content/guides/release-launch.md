## Launch facts

Active Matter launched on September 15, 2026 for Steam, Gaijin.Net PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The launch announcement also confirms Merits and a new Harmonization Cycle.

## Edition change

The former Premium Edition was renamed and upgraded to Deluxe at launch. See the editions page for the dated US Steam price and the official store link.
