## Short answer

Active Matter is an extraction shooter. You enter a quantum-unstable zone, collect Active Matter and other loot, then try to extract before the zone ceases to exist.

## A sensible first-raid plan

1. Choose a mode that matches your risk tolerance. Dedicated PvE and PvP options are officially supported.
2. Start with a small, understandable loadout. The official sources do not establish a universal “best” weapon or perk.
3. Treat every run as information gathering. Note the location name, mode and patch before trusting a community tip.
4. Extract with a modest haul. The official loop rewards returning with what you collected; exact extraction timers and coordinates are not verified here.

## What this guide does not claim

There is no verified launch-build evidence in the source set for fixed loot tables, exact boss windows, map-marker meanings or a universal first-raid route. Those claims stay out of this page until they can be reproduced.
