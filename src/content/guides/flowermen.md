## Confirmed behavior

Flowermen are plant-warped humans that hunt in packs. Nests have bright orange flowers. Flowermen can lie still until disturbed and can trap nearby Active Matter.

## Alpha Flowerman

The Gigastructure update introduced Alpha Flowerman. The official update confirms the name and addition; it does not establish a universal health value, timer or drop table in the source set.

## Safer reading of screenshots

Use the official Flowermen image set for identification. A generic creature screenshot is not enough to name a variant.
