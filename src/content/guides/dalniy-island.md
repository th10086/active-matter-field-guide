## Park

An overgrown Dalniy Island location with Flowermen, Dendroids and an abandoned laboratory below the greenhouse.

## Shegolskoe

An abandoned village on southern Dalniy Island where houses are suspended by gravitational anomalies.

## Dam

The former island power facility includes an administration building and high-value gear in the official description.

## Headquarters

A military and civilian complex with an airfield, Turned Soldiers and monsters. Exact routes and loot locations are not published here.
