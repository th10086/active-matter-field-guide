## Short answer

Look for Dendroids in **Park** on Dalniy Island. The greenhouse is Park's main landmark, and official articles describe both Flowermen and Dendroids as treating the area as their home. The developers recommend bringing **flamethrowers and incendiary grenades** when fighting through the location.

## Confirmed Dendroid location

| Detail | Officially confirmed |
|---|---|
| Map | Park |
| Main landmark | The greenhouse |
| Nearby point of interest | Underground facilities beneath the greenhouse |
| Suggested equipment | Flamethrowers and incendiary grenades |

Park lies between Shegolskoe and Dogorsk. Use the greenhouse as the reliable landmark when learning the map. For the wider location overview, see the [Park guide](/park/).

## How to prepare

The official Park preview specifically recommends fire-based equipment. Bring incendiary options before entering the overgrown area, keep space to move around large targets, and avoid treating a promotional screenshot as a guaranteed spawn marker.

## What is not officially confirmed

The official articles do not publish a fixed Dendroid spawn pin, respawn timer, drop rate or guaranteed route. Those details can change between raid variants and patches, so this guide does not invent coordinates or promise a repeatable farming path.
